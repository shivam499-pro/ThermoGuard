Terms of Use

Last updated: 20 September 2026

By opening the ThermoGuard dashboard or API you agree to use it as a research and demonstration tool only.

Not emergency services

Risk scores and tiers are investigation-priority / triage signals from a locked five-dimension model (30/25/20/10/15). They are not a validated probability of active combustion, wildfire, or industrial accident. Do not use ThermoGuard as the sole basis for evacuation, emergency dispatch, insurance denial, or legal claims.

Scientific limits

  • Sentinel-2 SWIR bands measure surface reflectance contrast, not temperature.
  • OpenStreetMap proximity is co-location evidence, not proof of industrial causation.
  • Missing OSM data does not prove industry is absent.
  • LOW risk plus LOW confidence does not mean a location is safe.

No warranty

The software and the 100-event pilot dataset are provided “as is” for hackathon and educational review. Satellite detections, OSM, and land-cover layers can be incomplete, delayed, or wrong.

API and dashboard

Public HTTPS endpoints are rate-limited by AWS. Do not attempt to overload the API. Do not scrape in a way that violates OpenStreetMap tile usage.
